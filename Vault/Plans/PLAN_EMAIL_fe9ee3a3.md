** Producer Price Index (PPI) – Friday
------------------------------------------------------------

In January, the PPI rose by 0.5% month-over-month, exceeding the forecast of 0.3%.
* Analysis: Points to accelerating price pressures at the production level. This stubborn inflation could eventually trickle down to consumer prices (CPI).
* Fed Perspective: Requires caution; persistent increases may stall the disinflation process and limit rate cuts.


** Consumer Confidence Index - Tuesday
------------------------------------------------------------

The index climbed to 91.2 points in February, higher than the expected 87.4.
* Analysis: Reflects improvement in sentiment and a positive outlook on the labor market. High confidence supports private consumption.
* Fed Perspective: Resilience is positive, but won't likely shift policy as long as overall inflation trends down.


** S&P/Case-Shiller Home Price Index - Tuesday
------------------------------------------------------------

The 20-city home price index rose by 1.4% annually in December.
* Analysis: Housing market stability despite high rates due to limited supply of existing homes.
* Fed Perspective: Fed remains watchful; housing costs are a major component of inflation metrics.
